# Charm Design Map (generated)

Authoritative mapping for the charm restoration. Generated by

Design decisions (user-settled 2026-07-20, 1:1 deterministic model):

- One buff per charm skill (1:1); buff name and icon match the granting item
  (trio items excepted: their placeholder art is replaced by classic charm icons).
- Deterministic only. Named charms = classic tier-3 (Major) values; greater
  charms = classic tier-4 values; `method=3` multipliers (verified on dev).
- Onslaught/Ethereal/Sanguine I-IV are kind-scoped bundles: every family of the
  kind in one buff at HALF the classic tier bonus. Broad-but-weak vs the focused
  single-family charms; the one-buff-per-kind slot rule makes it a tradeoff.
- Service charms (6/7/8, 31/32/33) share their line's tier-IV bundle skill.
- Trios fill all 3 kind slots: Balder's (81210) and Talisman (201000+) keep
  classic full tier-4 values (scarcity-gated); 98251 grants the 3 Major named
  buffs. Open balance knob: availability, not values.
- Duration 30 minutes; buffs persist through death (`notCareDeath`).
- Kinds: offense 9814, defense 9813, auxiliary 9812; level = tier (equal-or-
  higher replaces, lower does not override).
- Burn burst visuals: classic per-kind `targetEffectId` as `appearEffectId` on
  `FXBottom` (offense 10045001, defense 500245, auxiliary 50171).
- Spike abnormalities 488000003-488000006 deleted in 16-charm-skills, which also
  rewrites the 3 hand-edited spike skills to the clean pattern.
- Skill targeting: user + nearby allies (`allyExceptMe` r500 maxCount 19 + `me`
  r30), the BHS party food-buff pattern (Bleakfields BBQ skill 60950532).
